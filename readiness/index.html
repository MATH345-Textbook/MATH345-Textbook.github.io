Readiness checks

Before you start a unit, check that the ideas it builds on are solid. Each check adapts to your answers, tells you exactly what to review, and gives you a tutor pack to continue with Google Gemini. It runs entirely in your browser; your answers are not saved or sent anywhere.

Unit 2 readiness check

Anatomy of a linear map
Big question: Given a linear map, how do systems of equations reveal what it produces, what it forgets, and when it can be reversed? · About 9–15 questions.

Unit 3 readiness check

Beyond linear maps: local linearization and first-order optimization
Big question: If a function is not linear, what linear map best describes it near one point? How can that local model guide a step that decreases a scalar-valued function? · About 16–25 questions.

Unit 4 readiness check

Orthogonality and least squares
Big question: When $A\mathbf x=\mathbf b$ has no solution, what is the best approximate solution? · About 17–25 questions.

Unit 5 readiness check

Eigenvalues and second-order optimization
Big question: Which directions make a matrix simple, and how do they describe quadratic forms and the local shape of a function? · About 18–30 questions.
