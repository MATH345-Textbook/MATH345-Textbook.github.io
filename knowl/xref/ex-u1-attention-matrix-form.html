Example 1.3.12. The attention activity in matrix form.
In Activity 1.1.36, we computed the scores
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}}\cdot\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{small}},\qquad \mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}}\cdot\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{red}},\qquad \mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}}\cdot\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{bird}} \end{equation*}
one at a time. Matrix-vector multiplication computes the same scores at once.
🔗
Put the key vectors as the rows of
\begin{equation*} K=\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{small}}^T\\ \mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{red}}^T\\ \mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{bird}}^T \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
The matrix \(K\) collects the already-computed key vectors. Later, we will see how a learned weight matrix \(W_K\) produces these vectors from the token representations. Then
\begin{equation*} K\mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}}= \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{small}}\cdot\mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}}\\ \mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{red}}\cdot\mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}}\\ \mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{bird}}\cdot\mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}} \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
🔗
For the numbers in Activity 1.1.36,
\begin{equation*} K=\begin{bmatrix}1\amp0\\0\amp1\\1\amp1\end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}, \end{equation*}
so
\begin{equation*} K\mathbf{q}_{\text{bird}}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\\2\end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Next, divide each positive score by their sum, \(1+1+2=4\text{,}\) to obtain the attention weights
\begin{equation*} \boldsymbol{\alpha} =\begin{bmatrix}\alpha_{\text{small}}\\\alpha_{\text{red}}\\\alpha_{\text{bird}}\end{bmatrix} =\frac14\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\\2\end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix}1/4\\1/4\\1/2\end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Put the value vectors from Activity 1.1.36 in the rows of
\begin{equation*} V=\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{v}_{\text{small}}^T\\ \mathbf{v}_{\text{red}}^T\\ \mathbf{v}_{\text{bird}}^T \end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix}4\amp0\\0\amp4\\4\amp4\end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
Multiplying \(V\) on the left by the row vector \(\boldsymbol{\alpha}^T\) forms the weighted average of its rows:
\begin{align*} \mathbf{h}_{\text{bird}}^T \amp=\boldsymbol{\alpha}^T V\\ \amp=\alpha_{\text{small}}\mathbf{v}_{\text{small}}^T+\alpha_{\text{red}}\mathbf{v}_{\text{red}}^T+\alpha_{\text{bird}}\mathbf{v}_{\text{bird}}^T\\ \amp=\begin{bmatrix}1/4\amp1/4\amp1/2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}4\amp0\\0\amp4\\4\amp4\end{bmatrix}\\ \amp=\begin{bmatrix}3\amp3\end{bmatrix}. \end{align*}
Equivalently,
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{h}_{\text{bird}}=V^T\boldsymbol{\alpha}=\begin{bmatrix}3\\3\end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
This is the attention output at the bird position.
