Definition 6.2.2. Constrained critical point.

Let \(f,g:\R^n\to\R\) be differentiable, and let
\begin{equation*} S=\{\mathbf{x}\in\R^n:g(\mathbf{x})=k\}. \end{equation*}
Assume
\begin{equation*} \nabla g(\mathbf{x})\neq\mathbf{0} \end{equation*}
for every \(\mathbf{x}\in S\text{.}\) A point \(\mathbf{a}\in S\) in the interior of the domain of \(f\) is a constrained critical point of \(f\) on \(S\) if there exists \(\lambda\in\R\) such that
\begin{equation*} \nabla f(\mathbf{a})=\lambda\nabla g(\mathbf{a}). \end{equation*}
The scalar \(\lambda\) is a Lagrange multiplier.
