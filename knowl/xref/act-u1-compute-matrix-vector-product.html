Activity 1.3.1. Computing a matrix-vector product.

Compute \(A\mathbf{x}\) where
\begin{equation*} A=\begin{bmatrix}-1 \amp 4 \amp -5\\ 3 \amp 1 \amp -2\end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mathbf{x}=\begin{bmatrix}2\\-3\\4\end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Solution.
Using columns,
\begin{align*} A\mathbf{x} \amp=2\begin{bmatrix}-1\\3\end{bmatrix}-3\begin{bmatrix}4\\1\end{bmatrix}+4\begin{bmatrix}-5\\-2\end{bmatrix}\\ \amp=\begin{bmatrix}2(-1)-3(4)+4(-5)\\2(3)-3(1)+4(-2)\end{bmatrix}\\ \amp=\begin{bmatrix}-34\\-5\end{bmatrix}. \end{align*}
