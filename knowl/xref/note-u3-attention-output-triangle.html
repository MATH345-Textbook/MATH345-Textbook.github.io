Note 3.5.27. Softmax outputs stay in the value triangle.

Changing \(\mathbf{q}\) changes the score vector, and softmax adjusts the three weights together, but they stay positive and add to \(1\text{.}\) Therefore, the output always lies strictly inside the triangle with vertices \(\mathbf{v}_1\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{v}_2\text{,}\) and \(\mathbf{v}_3\text{.}\)
