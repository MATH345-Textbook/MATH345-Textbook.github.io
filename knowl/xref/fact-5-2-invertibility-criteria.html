Theorem 2.5.19. Invertibility Criteria.

Let \(A\) be an \(n \times n\) matrix. The following are equivalent:
🔗
  1. \(A\) is nonsingular/invertible.
    🔗
  2. The columns of \(A\) are linearly independent.
    🔗
  3. The columns of \(A\) span \(\R^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
  4. The rows of \(A\) are linearly independent.
    🔗
  5. The rows of \(A\) span \(\R^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
  6. \(A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}\) has only the trivial solution.
    🔗
  7. \(A\) is row equivalent to \(I_n\text{.}\)
    🔗
  8. The linear system \(A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}\) has a unique solution for every \(n \times 1\) matrix \(\mathbf{b}\text{.}\)
    🔗
  9. The rank of \(A\) is \(n\text{.}\)
    🔗
