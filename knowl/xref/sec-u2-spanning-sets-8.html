Activity 2.3.27. Testing membership in a span (U2-LO4).

In \(\R^3\text{,}\) consider the two vectors
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{v}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \quad\text{and}\quad \mathbf{v}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}\text{.} \end{equation*}
Determine if the vector
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{w} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 5 \\ -7 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
belongs to \(\spans \{ \mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2 \}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
We need to determine if \(\mathbf{w}\) is a linear combination of the vectors \(\mathbf{v}_1\) and \(\mathbf{v}_2\text{,}\) i.e., if it is possible to find scalars \(a\) and \(b\) such that \(\mathbf{w} = a \mathbf{v}_1 + b \mathbf{v}_2\text{.}\) Comparing entries of the vectors in each side of the equation, we obtain a system of equations in \(a\) and \(b\text{,}\) i.e.,
\begin{align*} 2a + b \amp = 1\\ a - b \amp = 5\\ a + 3b \amp = -7 \end{align*}
🔗
Practice this system in The Elimination Scratchpad
 2 
The Elimination Scratchpad
🔗
Apply Gaussian elimination to the augmented matrix:
\begin{equation*} \left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 2 \amp 1 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp -1 \amp 5 \\ 1 \amp 3 \amp -7 \end{array}\right]. \end{equation*}
The first entry in column 1 is already nonzero. Following Algorithm 2.2.40, first scale row 1 to obtain a leading one, then eliminate the entries below it. Repeat this process on the remaining rows:
\begin{align*} R_1\to \tfrac{1}{2}R_1 \quad \amp \left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 1 \amp \tfrac{1}{2} \amp \tfrac{1}{2} \\ 1 \amp -1 \amp 5 \\ 1 \amp 3 \amp -7 \end{array}\right]\\ \substack{R_2\to R_2-R_1\\R_3\to R_3-R_1} \quad \amp \left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 1 \amp \tfrac{1}{2} \amp \tfrac{1}{2} \\ 0 \amp -\tfrac{3}{2} \amp \tfrac{9}{2} \\ 0 \amp \tfrac{5}{2} \amp -\tfrac{15}{2} \end{array}\right]\\ R_2\to -\tfrac{2}{3}R_2 \quad \amp \left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 1 \amp \tfrac{1}{2} \amp \tfrac{1}{2} \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp -3 \\ 0 \amp \tfrac{5}{2} \amp -\tfrac{15}{2} \end{array}\right]\\ R_3\to R_3-\tfrac{5}{2}R_2 \quad \amp \left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 1 \amp \tfrac{1}{2} \amp \tfrac{1}{2} \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp -3 \\ 0 \amp 0 \amp 0 \end{array}\right]. \end{align*}
🔗
The last row represents \(0=0\text{,}\) so it imposes no additional condition. The second row gives \(b=-3\text{.}\) Back substitution into the first row, \(a+\tfrac{1}{2}b=\tfrac{1}{2}\text{,}\) gives \(a=\tfrac{1}{2}-\tfrac{1}{2}b=2\text{.}\) Thus the system is consistent, with solution \(a=2\) and \(b=-3\text{.}\) Therefore \(\mathbf{w}=2\mathbf{v}_1-3\mathbf{v}_2\text{,}\) so \(\mathbf{w}\) belongs to \(\spans\{\mathbf{v}_1,\mathbf{v}_2\}\text{.}\)
