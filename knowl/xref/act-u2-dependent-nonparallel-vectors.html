Activity 2.4.6. Testing independence of three nonparallel vectors (U2-LO4).

Use Gaussian elimination to determine whether the vectors
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{v}_1=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mathbf{v}_2=\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\1\end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mathbf{v}_3=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\\2\end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
are linearly independent. If they are dependent, give a nontrivial linear combination of them that equals \(\mathbf{0}\text{.}\)

Solution.
We determine whether \(t_1\mathbf{v}_1+t_2\mathbf{v}_2+t_3\mathbf{v}_3=\mathbf{0}\) has a nontrivial solution. Comparing coordinates gives
\begin{align*} t_1+t_3\amp=0\\ t_2+t_3\amp=0\\ t_1+t_2+2t_3\amp=0. \end{align*}
The first two pivots are already \(1\text{,}\) so we only need to eliminate the entries below them:
\begin{align*} \amp\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1\amp0\amp1\amp0\\ 0\amp1\amp1\amp0\\ 1\amp1\amp2\amp0 \end{array}\right]\\ \amp\xrightarrow{R_3\to R_3-R_1} \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1\amp0\amp1\amp0\\ 0\amp1\amp1\amp0\\ 0\amp1\amp1\amp0 \end{array}\right]\\ \amp\xrightarrow{R_3\to R_3-R_2} \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1\amp0\amp1\amp0\\ 0\amp1\amp1\amp0\\ 0\amp0\amp0\amp0 \end{array}\right]. \end{align*}
🔗
The variable \(t_3\) is free. Setting \(t_3=s\) gives \(t_1=-s\) and \(t_2=-s\text{.}\) In particular, choosing \(s=1\) gives the nontrivial relation
\begin{equation*} -\mathbf{v}_1-\mathbf{v}_2+\mathbf{v}_3=\mathbf{0}. \end{equation*}
Thus the three vectors are linearly dependent, and \(\mathbf{v}_3=\mathbf{v}_1+\mathbf{v}_2\text{.}\)
