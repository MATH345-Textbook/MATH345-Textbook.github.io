Theorem 5.1.12.

Suppose \(A\) is a square matrix and \(\lambda \in \R\text{.}\) Then \(E_\lambda(A)\) is a subspace of \(\R^n\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Why is this true?.

Note that \(\mathbf{x} \in E_\lambda(A)\) precisely when \(\mathbf{x}\) solves the equation \((A - \lambda I) \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}\text{.}\) Thus \(E_\lambda(A)\) is precisely the null space of the matrix \(A - \lambda I\text{,}\) and the theorem follows from Theorem 2.3.18.
