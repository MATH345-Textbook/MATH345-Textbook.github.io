Algorithm 4.4.6. Least squares with \(QR\).

Input. An \(m\times n\) matrix \(A\) with linearly independent columns and a vector \(\mathbf{b}\in\mathbb{R}^m\text{.}\)
🔗
  1. Compute a reduced \(QR\) factorization
    \begin{equation*} A=QR. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Compute
    \begin{equation*} \mathbf{y}=Q^T\mathbf{b}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Solve the upper-triangular system
    \begin{equation*} R\widehat{\mathbf{x}}=\mathbf{y} \end{equation*}
    by back-substitution.
    🔗
    🔗
Output. The vector \(\widehat{\mathbf{x}}\) is the least-squares coefficient vector. The fitted vector is
\begin{equation*} A\widehat{\mathbf{x}} = QR\widehat{\mathbf{x}} = QQ^T\mathbf{b}. \end{equation*}
