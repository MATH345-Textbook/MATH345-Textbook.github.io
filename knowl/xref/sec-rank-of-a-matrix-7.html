Note 2.4.37.

If \(A\) is row equivalent to a row echelon matrix \(R\text{,}\) then \(\row(A)=\row(R)\text{.}\) By Lemma 2.4.36, the nonzero rows of \(R\) form a basis for this space. Thus \(\dimens(\row(A))\) equals the number of nonzero rows, or equivalently the number of pivots, in \(R\text{.}\) In particular, this pivot count is independent of the choice of row echelon form.
