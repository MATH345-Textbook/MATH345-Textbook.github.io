Activity 2.4.51. Revisiting the height map with rank and nullity (U2-LO3).

Return again to Activity 2.1.1. Let
\begin{equation*} P= \begin{bmatrix} 1\amp0\amp0\\ 0\amp1\amp0 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
Find the column space, null space, rank, and nullity of \(P\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
The column space is the span of the columns of \(P\text{.}\) Its first two columns are the standard basis vectors of \(\mathbb R^2\text{,}\) and its third column is zero, which adds no new vectors to the span. Therefore
\begin{equation*} \col(P) = \spans\left\{ \begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix} \right\} = \mathbb R^2, \end{equation*}
so \(\rank(P)=2\text{.}\)
🔗
To find the null space, solve \(P\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{0}\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} P\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\x_2\\x_3\end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\x_2\end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
Thus \(x_1=x_2=0\text{,}\) while \(x_3\) is free. Setting \(x_3=t\) gives every solution as
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{x}=\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\t\end{bmatrix} =t\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{bmatrix},\qquad t\in\mathbb R. \end{equation*}
Hence
\begin{equation*} \nulls(P) = \spans\left\{ \begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{bmatrix} \right\}, \end{equation*}
so \(\nullity(P)=1\text{.}\)
