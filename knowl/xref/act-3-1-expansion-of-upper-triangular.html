Activity 2.5.35. The determinant of a triangular matrix (U2-LO7).

Compute \(\det(A)\text{,}\) where
\begin{equation*} A = \begin{bmatrix} a \amp b \amp c \\ 0 \amp d \amp e \\ 0 \amp 0 \amp f \end{bmatrix}\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Solution.
Expanding in cofactors along column \(1\) (because most of the entries of that column are zeros), we find
\begin{align*} \det(A) \amp = a \operatorname{cof}(A)_{11} + 0 \operatorname{cof}(A)_{21}\\ \amp\quad + 0 \operatorname{cof}(A)_{31}\\ \amp = a (-1)^{1+1} \det \begin{bmatrix} d \amp e \\ 0 \amp f \end{bmatrix}\\ \amp = a ((d)(f) - (e)(0))\\ \amp = (a)(d)(f)\\ \amp = adf. \end{align*}
