Fact 2.3.20. General solution of a linear system.

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix. If \(\mathbf{x}_p\) is one solution of \(A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \{\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb R^n:A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}\}=\mathbf{x}_p+\nulls(A). \end{equation*}
That is, the solutions are exactly the vectors \(\mathbf{x}_p+\mathbf{z}\) with \(\mathbf{z}\in\nulls(A)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Why is this true?.

Since \(A\mathbf{x}_p=\mathbf{b}\text{,}\) for any \(\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb R^n\text{,}\)
\begin{align*} A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}\amp\Longleftrightarrow A(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_p)=\mathbf{0}\\ \amp\Longleftrightarrow \mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_p\in\nulls(A). \end{align*}
Thus \(A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}\) exactly when \(\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{x}_p+\mathbf{z}\) for some \(\mathbf{z}\in\nulls(A)\text{.}\)
