Theorem 3.4.6. Continuity of First Partials Implies Differentiability.
Let \(f\) be a scalar-valued function, and suppose \(\mathbf{a}\) is an interior point (recall Definition 3.1.28) of the domains of \(f\) and its partial derivatives. Moreover, suppose that all of the partial derivatives \(D_1 f, \dots, D_n f\) are continuous at \(\mathbf{a}\text{.}\) Then \(f\) is differentiable at \(\mathbf{a}\text{.}\)
