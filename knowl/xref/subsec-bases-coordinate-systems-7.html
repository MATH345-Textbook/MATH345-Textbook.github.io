Definition 2.4.29. Coordinate Vectors With Respect to a Basis.

Let \(U\) be an \(m\)-dimensional subspace of \(\R^n\text{,}\) and let \(B = (\mathbf{b}_1,\dots,\mathbf{b}_m)\) be an ordered basis for \(U\text{.}\) By Theorem 2.4.9, given any vector \(\mathbf{v} \in U\text{,}\) there exist unique scalars \(t_1,\dots,t_m\) such that
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{v} = t_1 \mathbf{b}_1 + \cdots + t_m \mathbf{b}_m \end{equation*}
and we define the coordinate vector of a vector \(\mathbf{v} \in U\) with respect to \(B\) to be the \(m\)-vector
\begin{equation*} C_B(\mathbf{v}) = \begin{bmatrix} t_1 \\ \vdots \\ t_m \end{bmatrix}\text{.} \end{equation*}
