Theorem 4.2.22. Properties of the Gram-Schmidt output.

Apply Algorithm 4.2.20 to a matrix \(A=[\mathbf{a}_1\ \cdots\ \mathbf{a}_n]\) with linearly independent columns, and let \(U=\spans\{\mathbf{a}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{a}_n\}\text{.}\) For each \(k=1,\ldots,n\text{,}\) write \(U_k=\spans\{\mathbf{a}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{a}_k\}\text{.}\) The output through step \(k\) satisfies:
🔗
  • The span is preserved:
    \begin{equation*} \spans\{\mathbf{v}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{v}_k\}=\spans\{\mathbf{q}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{q}_k\}=U_k. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  • The normalized vectors \(\{\mathbf{q}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{q}_k\}\) form an orthonormal basis of \(U_k\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
In particular, \(\{\mathbf{q}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{q}_n\}\) is an orthonormal basis of \(U\text{.}\)
