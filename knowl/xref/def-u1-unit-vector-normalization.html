Definition 1.1.11. Unit vectors and normalization.

A unit vector is a vector of length one. If \(\mathbf{x}\) is nonzero, the unit vector in the direction of \(\mathbf{x}\) is
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{u}=\frac{\mathbf{x}}{\|\mathbf{x}\|}. \end{equation*}
Dividing a nonzero vector by its norm is called normalization. It preserves the vector’s direction and gives it length one.
