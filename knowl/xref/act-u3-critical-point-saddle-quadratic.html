Activity 3.8.2.
Find the critical points of \(f(x,y) = x^2 - y^2\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
We compute that \(f_x(x,y) = 2x\) and \(f_y(x,y) = -2y\text{.}\) The only \((x,y)\) such that \(f_x(x,y) = 2x = 0\) and \(f_y(x,y) = -2y = 0\) is \((0,0)\text{.}\) This is the only critical point.
