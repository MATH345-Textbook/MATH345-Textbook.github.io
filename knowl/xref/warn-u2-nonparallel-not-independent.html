Warning 2.4.7. Nonparallel vectors can still be dependent.

In Activity 2.4.6, the third vector is not parallel to either of the first two: its second entry is \(1\text{,}\) while every scalar multiple of \(\mathbf{v}_1\) has second entry \(0\text{;}\) its first entry is \(1\text{,}\) while every scalar multiple of \(\mathbf{v}_2\) has first entry \(0\text{.}\) The first two vectors are also not parallel. Nevertheless, the three vectors are dependent because \(\mathbf{v}_3=\mathbf{v}_1+\mathbf{v}_2\text{.}\) For three or more vectors, checking that no pair is parallel is not enough to establish linear independence: a vector can be a linear combination of the others without being a scalar multiple of any one of them.
