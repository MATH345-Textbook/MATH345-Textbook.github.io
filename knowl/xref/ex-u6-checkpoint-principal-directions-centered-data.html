Additional exercise 6.8.10. Principal directions of a centered data set.

The rows of
\begin{equation*} X= \begin{bmatrix} 4\amp2\\ 2\amp4\\ -2\amp0\\ 0\amp-2 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
are four data points in \(\R^2\text{.}\)
🔗
  1. Compute the mean \(\bar{\mathbf{x}}\) and the centered data matrix \(Z\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Compute the covariance matrix
    \begin{equation*} C=\frac14Z^TZ. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Find the eigenvalues of \(C\) and corresponding orthonormal eigenvectors. Choose \(\mathbf{v}_1\) for the larger eigenvalue and \(\mathbf{v}_2\) for the smaller eigenvalue.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Form
    \begin{equation*} V= \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{v}_1\amp\mathbf{v}_2 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
    and compute the principal-coordinate matrix
    \begin{equation*} T=ZV. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Compute
    \begin{equation*} V^TCV. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  6. What fraction of the total centered variation is captured by the first principal direction?
    🔗
    🔗
