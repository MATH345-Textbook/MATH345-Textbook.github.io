Theorem 1.1.11. Angle between vectors.

For two nonzero vectors \(\mathbf{v}, \mathbf{w} \in \R^n\text{,}\) the angle \(\theta\) between the vectors is the quantity satisfying the equation
\begin{equation*} \cos \theta = \frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w}}{\| \mathbf{v} \| \| \mathbf{w} \|}\text{.} \end{equation*}
